What is Nostr?

Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays

Nostr is a revolutionary protocol that enables truly decentralized social networks and communication platforms. Unlike traditional centralized platforms, Nostr ensures that no single entity can control, censor, or shut down your communications.

Cryptographic Identity

Your identity is based on cryptographic keys, not usernames controlled by corporations. You own your identity completely.

Lightning Integration

Built-in Bitcoin Lightning support enables instant, low-cost micropayments and tips directly within chat applications.

Global Relay Network

Messages are distributed across multiple relays worldwide, ensuring redundancy and resistance to censorship.

Interoperability

Switch between different Nostr clients while keeping your identity, followers, and message history intact.

Censorship Resistant

No central authority can ban accounts, delete messages, or control what you can say or share.

Cross-Platform

Access your messages and connections from any device - desktop, web, or mobile - with seamless synchronization.

Why Nostr Matters for Chat Applications

Traditional messaging platforms can read your messages, ban your account, or disappear overnight. With Nostr-based chat applications, you control your data, choose your relays, and communicate freely without intermediaries. It's time to take back control of digital communication.

Technology Stack

Chat Application Architecture

Multi-platform chat applications built on the Nostr protocol with modern frameworks and real-time communication.

Technical Implementation:

  • Desktop: Avalonia UI with C# for native cross-platform desktop experience across Windows, macOS, and Linux.
  • Web: React-based Progressive Web App with service workers for offline functionality.
  • Mobile: React Native for native iOS and Android performance with shared codebase.
  • Real-time Sync: WebSocket connections to multiple Nostr relays for redundancy and speed.

Security & Privacy

Advanced cryptographic security with user-controlled privacy settings and optional end-to-end encryption.

Security Features:

  • Key Management: Secure storage of Nostr private keys with biometric protection on mobile.
  • Message Encryption: Optional NIP-04 direct message encryption with forward secrecy.
  • Relay Selection: User-controlled relay management with privacy-focused defaults.
  • Local Storage: Encrypted local database for offline message access and caching.

Lightning Integration

Native Bitcoin Lightning Network integration for seamless micropayments and economic spam prevention.

Payment Features:

  • Wallet Integration: Support for LND, CLN, and web-based Lightning wallets.
  • In-Chat Payments: Send and receive sats directly within conversations.
  • Economic Anti-Spam: Optional micropayment requirements for unsolicited messages.
  • Group Monetization: Paid group access and premium features for content creators.
